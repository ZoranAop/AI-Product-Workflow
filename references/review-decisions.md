# Review and Decision Recording

After every important review, record the decision and its background — never just the result.

## Minimum Review Record

```
Date:
Participants:

Background:
Problem:

Candidate options:

Final decision:

Decision reasons:

Rejected options:

Impact scope:

Follow-up actions:

Confirmer:
```

## Rules

- Never tell the Agent only "delete this feature". Always record why, based on what information at the time, and under what conditions it could be reconsidered.
- Decisions live in `03-planning/decision-log.md`.
- When a decision is reversed, append a superseding entry; do not delete history.
- A review without a recorded decision + background is an incomplete stage; the stage gate does not pass.

## Development Sync

Any scheme change during development must be written back. Stale context makes even a strong model useless. See `assets/rules/change-management.md`.
