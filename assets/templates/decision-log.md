# Decision Log

Cross-requirement decisions, append-only.

Rules:
- Never delete an entry. When a decision is reversed, append a new entry that supersedes it.
- Every entry records the decision AND its background (when / who / why).
- If a decision was made in a meeting, record it before the meeting ends or within the agreed window.

## Template

### YYYY-MM-DD — <short decision title>

- Participants: <names>
- Background: <what was known at the time>
- Problem: <the question being decided>
- Options considered: <A / B / …>
- Decision: <chosen option>
- Reasons: <why>
- Rejected: <what was dropped and why>
- Impact: <scope>
- Reconsider if: <conditions under which this can be reopened>
- Supersedes: <earlier entry, if any>
